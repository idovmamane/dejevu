# Code of conduct

Be kind, be specific, bring numbers.

- Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, no personal attacks, no discrimination of any kind.
- Disagree with evidence: a trace, a measurement, a reproduction. Not with volume.
- Assume good faith. Ask before judging.
- Keep credit honest. Say what you measured and where the numbers come from.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and block participants who break these rules. To report a problem, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub.
